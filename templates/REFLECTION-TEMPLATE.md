# Reflection YYYY-MM-DD

## Coverage
Window mined, sessions, days with no report, and why (did the habit lapse?).

## Carried over
Last round's accepted items: built, or still missing. An unbuilt item outranks
every new candidate.

## Scoreboard
Output of `score`. Include the bad verdicts - they are the useful ones.

## Signals
One line each: count, shape, session id, a verbatim quote, and yours or theirs.

## Families
Signals grouped by the shape of the mistake, not by the file it happened in.

## Verdicts
Rung taken (GUARD / SKILL / RULE / NOTHING) and what actually changed on disk.
For every GUARD, paste the `prove` result.

## Not proposing
What looks like a problem but should not be built, and why.

## State
Files touched. What you verified. What you did NOT verify.
